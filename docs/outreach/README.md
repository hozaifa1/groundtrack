# Cold outreach — round 1

**Status: drafted, not sent.** These are ready to paste and send. Nothing here has
gone out, and no address below has been verified. Sending is a human action and is
deliberately not automated.

## Why this exists

The single biggest gap between this project and a Grand Prize entry is stated plainly
in the blueprint's §9: the named beneficiary — a small university CubeSat or NewSpace
ops team — is *plausible* but *unvalidated*. Implementation & Feasibility is a full
quarter of the judging rubric. One named person on record saying "yes, that would have
saved us time" is worth more than another point of F1.

## The ask, and why it is small

Fifteen minutes. Watch a screen recording, answer one question: **would this have saved
you time?** Not a partnership, not a pilot, not a data-sharing agreement. A question
that can be answered in one sentence gets answered; a question that implies a meeting
does not.

## What we may claim afterwards

- A reply saying it would have helped, quoted with permission and attributed → goes in
  the README and the video.
- A reply saying it would *not* have helped → also goes in the README. A stated,
  sourced negative is worth more to a skeptical judge than silence, and the blueprint
  already commits to reporting it either way.
- No reply → every claim implying operator validation gets dropped. No exceptions, no
  softening into "designed with input from".

## Before sending

1. Verify each address on the institution's own site. Do not guess a format.
2. Have the screen recording ready and linked before the first send. An email promising
   a recording that does not exist yet will not get a second reply.
3. Send individually. No BCC lists — a visibly bulk email answers the "is this person
   serious" question the wrong way.
4. Expect a low reply rate. The follow-up is already scheduled for Day 7.
