# AI Builders Challenge with IBM Bob: Competition Reference

## Key details
- **Competition**: AI Builders Challenge with IBM Bob (August Challenge & Wildcard)
- **Sponsor**: IBM SkillsBuild | **Organizer**: BeMyApp Corporation
- **Final submission deadline**: August 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM ET (September 1, 2026 at 09:59 AM Asia/Dhaka)
- **Total prize pool**: $15,000 USD
  - Grand Prize: $5,000 USD (across both challenges)
  - 1st Place: $2,250 USD
  - Runner Up: $1,250 USD
  - Best Technical Use of IBM Bob: $750 USD
  - Most Innovative Use of IBM Bob: $750 USD

## Active challenge themes
1. **Advance Space Exploration with AI (August theme)**:
   - Build systems that turn space telemetry and mission data into operational insights for spacecraft exploration.
2. **Build Intelligent Systems for the Future of Work (Wildcard theme)**:
   - Build automation and decision support tooling for distributed enterprise workflows.

## Mandatory submission requirements
- [x] **1. Complete learning activity**: IBM SkillsBuild prerequisite course.
- [x] **2. Core IBM Bob usage**: Core component must use IBM Bob (optional additions: watsonx, Granite, LangFlow, Docling).
- [x] **3. Public GitHub repository**: Clean architecture, documented source code, and proper `.gitignore`.
- [x] **4. Clear README**: Document the problem, technical approach, real-world impact, and IBM Bob usage.
- [x] **5. Functioning prototype**: Runnable proof of concept (CLI, API, or web interface). Live at https://groundtrack-console.vercel.app
- [ ] **6. Solution presentation video**: Demo video of up to 3 minutes (YouTube, Loom, or Vimeo).
- [ ] **7. Event platform submission**: Complete project submission on BeMyApp before August 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM ET.
